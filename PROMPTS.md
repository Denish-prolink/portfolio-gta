# Anime engineer city backgrounds

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, one separate call per scene. Original PNGs are saved in `art-source/`; optimized images consumed by the site are in `public/art/`. These replace all nine previous backgrounds.

The character wears a charcoal bomber jacket, cream T-shirt, dark cargo trousers and white sneakers. Each scene uses an anime character with a GTA-inspired coastal city atmosphere, leaving the left side quiet for portfolio copy. Images were generated from text prompts; a local reference-image attempt was rejected by the tool.

## Final prompts

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-screen 16:9 software engineer portfolio background, 1536x1024 or wider. Create an original anime illustration with the cinematic city atmosphere and bold outlined, painted loading-screen aesthetic of GTA. Scene: a rooftop software engineering studio with an open laptop on a desk beside a terrace overlooking a palm-lined coastal city, distant towers, streets and ocean at golden hour. Subject: one clearly recognizable adult anime young man, age 25, short tousled black hair, warm medium skin, clean shaven, wearing an oversized charcoal bomber jacket over a cream T-shirt, dark cargo trousers and white sneakers. He sits at the laptop in three-quarter view on the RIGHT third, thoughtful confident expression. Show enough face to read as anime. Clothing is contemporary GTA-style streetwear. Left 55 percent must be uncluttered dark architectural shadow and hazy distant city, reserved for white webpage text. Keep character's head near 73 percent across and 44 percent down so webpage HUD does not cover it. Cinematic wide environmental view, finely inked anime contours, cel shading with painterly scenery, warm orange coral skyline and deep charcoal foreground. No lettering, no logos, no watermarks, no UI, no guns. Single landscape artwork, not collage.
```
